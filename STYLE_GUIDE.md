# Telic Vision Style Guide

![Telic Vision monogram](telic-vision-logo.jpg)

## Brand identity

Telic Vision should feel:

- **Authoritative:** Built for consequential enterprise work.
- **Precise:** Every action appears deliberate and controlled.
- **Institutional:** More private atelier or certification authority than consumer SaaS.
- **Intelligent:** Sophisticated without relying on familiar “AI” imagery.
- **Quietly premium:** Luxury expressed through typography, space, and restraint.
- **Trustworthy:** Quality, security, and verification are visible throughout the experience.

The visual direction combines an editorial luxury brand with a technical control system.

## Color palette

### Primary colors

- **Black — `#000000`**  
  Primary buttons, active states, progress, typography, and the logo.

- **White — `#FFFFFF`**  
  Main background and reversed text.

- **Ink — `#111111`**  
  Softer alternative to pure black for body copy.

### Supporting neutrals

- **Strong rule — `#222222`**  
  Important input and container borders.

- **Muted gray — `#6B6B6B`**  
  Supporting copy, metadata, and inactive controls.

- **Stone — `#A3A3A3`**  
  Placeholders, counters, and tertiary information.

- **Hairline — `#E5E5E5`**  
  Dividers, grid lines, and quiet borders.

- **Canvas — `#FAFAFA`**  
  Subtle surface differentiation.

- **Preview surround — `#ECECEC`**  
  Workspace background behind generated applications.

The Telic Vision interface should remain almost entirely monochrome. Colors inside generated applications—such as the clinic’s teal palette—belong to the generated customer brand, not Telic Vision itself.

## Typography

### Cinzel

Use for brand names, major headings, certification scores, and formal titles.

- Weights: 400, 600, 700
- Standard weight: 600
- Uppercase
- Letter spacing: `0.15em`
- Character: classical, prestigious, architectural

Examples:

- TELIC VISION
- AUTONOMOUS COUNCIL
- TELIC QUALITY CERTIFICATION

### Montserrat

Use for navigation, buttons, labels, statuses, metadata, and operational controls.

- Weights: 500, 600, 700
- Standard weight: 600
- Uppercase
- Letter spacing: `0.18em–0.30em`
- Typical size: `8–11px`
- Character: technical, controlled, contemporary

### Inter

Use for paragraphs, descriptions, inputs, and longer interface text.

- Weights: 400, 500, 600
- Base size: `15px`
- Line height: `1.6`
- Letter spacing: `0.01em`
- Character: neutral, readable, functional

### Nunito

Reserved for generated application previews. It should not appear in the main Telic Vision interface.

## Logo

The logo is an overlapping serif **TV monogram** in pure black.

Usage rules:

- Display primarily in black on white.
- Preserve the original proportions.
- Never stretch, rotate, outline, recolor, or add effects.
- Avoid gradients, shadows, and decorative containers.
- Use the compact `36 × 36px` presentation in application navigation.
- Pair it with the Cinzel “TELIC VISION” wordmark where space allows.

## Layout and spacing

- Favor large areas of white space.
- Use centered, restrained compositions for major decisions.
- Use structured grids for technical and operational information.
- Separate sections with thin `1px` hairlines.
- Keep content widths controlled; avoid text spanning the entire viewport.
- Use an approximately `8px` spacing system.
- Prefer square panels and controls over heavily rounded cards.

The main intake screen should feel spacious and contemplative. Execution and certification screens should become denser and more systematic.

## Components

### Buttons

Primary buttons:

- Black background
- White uppercase Montserrat
- Wide letter spacing
- No shadow
- Subtle hover opacity of approximately `0.88`

Most buttons should have square corners. The main “Start the Build” action may use a pill shape to distinguish the product’s primary moment of commitment.

Secondary buttons:

- White background
- `1px` black border
- Black text

### Inputs

- White background
- Strong `1px` border
- Generous internal padding
- Inter body type
- Minimal decoration
- Stone-colored placeholder text

### Status indicators

- Use bordered monochrome labels.
- Inactive states appear muted.
- Active states use stronger black borders.
- Completed states invert to white text on black.
- Motion should communicate activity without becoming theatrical.

### Dividers and cards

- Prefer hairline borders over shadows.
- Avoid floating, rounded SaaS cards.
- Use grid alignment and whitespace to establish hierarchy.

## Motion

Motion should be subtle and functional:

- Fade transitions around `200–300ms`
- State changes around `160ms`
- Progress transitions around `400ms`
- Gentle opacity pulses for live execution
- No bouncing, elastic motion, or decorative animation

## Brand voice

The writing should be concise, assured, and outcome-oriented.

Preferred vocabulary:

- Build
- Artifact
- Harness
- Council
- Certified
- Verified
- Production
- Dossier
- Synthesis
- Released

Avoid:

- Excessive AI hype
- Casual startup slang
- Emojis
- Exclamation marks
- Claims such as “magic,” “revolutionary,” or “instant”

Telic Vision should sound like a capable institution documenting verified work—not a chatbot trying to impress the user.
